## The problem

Before a structural engineering firm issues a drawing set, its engineers check it by hand against code requirements and the firm's own standards. It's slow, and small misses come back later as plan-check comments.

## What I built

- Automated checks for structural drawing sets, written with the firm's licensed engineers.
- An LLM (Claude API) that turns architect plans, soils reports and plan-check letters into structured data and draft responses.
- Every value the model reads is tied to its source page, and engineers review and sign off on everything.

- Caught 101 of 107 known issues (94%) across 83 archived projects.
